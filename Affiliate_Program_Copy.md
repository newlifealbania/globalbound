## Recommended program settings (your call — set these in Lemon Squeezy)

- **Commission: 30% recurring.** Generous enough to attract real promoters; on the $24/year plan that's about $7.20 per referral, every year they stay subscribed. (Anywhere from 20–40% is reasonable — higher recruits harder-working affiliates.)
- **Cookie window: 60 days** (or the longest Lemon Squeezy offers). If someone clicks an affiliate's link and subscribes within the window, the affiliate gets credit. A longer window is friendlier to affiliates and standard for subscriptions.
- **Payout: automatic.** Lemon Squeezy handles affiliate payouts for you — hands-off, and affiliates see their earnings in their own dashboard.
- **Approval: manual to start.** Review each applicant so you keep your program aligned with the expat/nomad niche and avoid spammy sign-ups. You can switch to auto-approve later.
- **Budget note:** Lemon Squeezy adds a 2% platform fee on affiliate-referred sales (on top of the commission you set). At 30% commission that's ~35.9% total cost on those sales — still healthy, just factor it in if you consider going above 30%.

## Note on discount codes (do this once your store is live)

For influencer deals, pair each creator with **both** a unique referral link *and* a memorable discount code (e.g. `NINA15` for 15% off). The code is what actually gets used in videos and reels — people rarely tap links from video — and it doubles as the creator's attribution. It also gives their audience a reason to buy now.
