Delete Your Account

Global Bound · Global Bound LLC

This page explains how to delete your Global Bound account and the data associated with it. You can delete your account yourself in seconds inside the app, or ask us to delete it for you.

Deleting your account is permanent. Once your data is removed it cannot be recovered. If you would like a copy first, you can export everything from Settings → Your data before deleting.

Option 1: Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Global Bound and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon in the top corner).
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account & data and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all of your budget data.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you cannot access the app, email us from the email address on your account and we will delete it for you:

Email newlifealbania@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Global Bound account."

We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the data tied to it, including:

  • Your account email and sign-in credentials.
  • All of your budget content: income, expenses, budgets, categories, savings goals, trips, places, visa and stay trackers, and currency preferences.

What may be retained, and for how long

After deletion we do not keep your budget data. A limited amount of information may be retained only where we are legally required to, for example basic transaction records held by our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy) for tax and accounting purposes. These records are kept only as long as the law requires and are not used for any other purpose. We never sell your data.
